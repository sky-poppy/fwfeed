External lists have their own overlap quantity but the lists can be used for different security requirements for any
administrative purposes. We use these external lists as measurement tools to evaluate the performance of the honeypot dataset.

Note: Any lists with CIDR will be flattened and private networks removed from the external lists so the qty of IP found are internet IPs and not millions of privately addressed IPs.

The re-use of IP information across various blocklists is not providing good protection, the amount of energy spend on reporting and tracking for existing lists
appears to exceeds the energy used to collect new bad actor IP information based on honeypot dataset results.
