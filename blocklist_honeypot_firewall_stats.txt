Repeating = IP already exists in honeypot dataset, qty of unique IP re-visiting the honeypot.

External = IP does not exist in honeypot dataset or predictive lists, but does exist in a external list.
Unknown/New = IP does not exist in honeypot dataset, honeypot predictive lists or external lists at the time of logging. IP added to the honeypot dataset
