This honeypot has grown through direct data collection only via honeypots we operate.
IPs found in other blocklists showing below is expected due to bad actor trends to search for multiple internet hosts.
As per our policy method, we recommend you combine blocklists to match your own security requirements.
Bad actor IPs looking at webserver appliance has logged IP information as webserver honeypot was triggered for requests of unknown files from the webhost. Examples aa.php filemanager.php etc.
Bad actor IPs looking at firewall appliance has logged IP information when firewall honeypot is accessed including port scans regardless if tcp or udp items.

External lists have their own overlap quantity but the lists can be used for different security requirements for any
administrative purposes. We use these external lists as measurement tools to evaluate the performance of the honeypot.

Note: Any lists with CIDR will be flattened and private networks removed from the external lists so the qty of IP found are internet IPs and not millions of privately addressed IPs.
Many of these lists are sandwiched with the IP ingredients from other lists to try and simplify blocklist use. Examples: Firehol, Bitwire, Romainmarcoux lists.
The re-use of IP information across various blocklists is not providing good protection, the amount of energy spend on reporting and tracking for existing lists far
exceeds the energy or effort used to collect new bad actor IP information.
